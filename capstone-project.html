Overview

About the Project

In-Eternity is a side-scrolling action RPG where your emotions are your greatest weapon. You play as a girl trapped in a surreal dreamscape — a world built from forgotten memories and suppressed emotions. Your quest is not just to escape, but to rediscover who you are by confronting the echoes of your past.

As a Game Developer and 2D Artist on the project, I contributed to the game's visual direction and implementation — from concept work and 2D art assets to supporting the gameplay and systems that bring the dreamscape to life. The project is built in Unreal Engine and is currently in active development.

Features

Key Features

🗺️ Non-Linear Dreamscape

Explore a surreal world built from forgotten memories and uncover its secrets at your own pace.

🔀 Dual Combat Modes

Battle through dedicated arena encounters or engage enemies in the exploration map.

🧠 Emotion-Based Dialogue

Choose responses through a counseling-inspired system — Happy, Anger, Calm, Sad — and shape your story.

🧩 Memory Progression

Recover memories to unlock new skills and abilities tied to the emotions you rediscover.

⚙️ Custom Playstyle

Build your character around the emotions and memories you choose to recover.

🎮 Gamepad & Keyboard

Playable with an X-Input compatible controller or keyboard and mouse — gamepad recommended.
